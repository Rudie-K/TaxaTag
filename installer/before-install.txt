TaxaTag - environmental DNA to species

This installs the TaxaTag application, about 430 MB.

IT DOES NOT INSTALL A REFERENCE LIBRARY.

A reference library is the collection of known sequences TaxaTag compares your
data against. It is around 2 GB, it is data rather than program, and it is
downloaded separately - from inside TaxaTag, once it is running.

You do not need one to check that TaxaTag works. Press "Test the analysis
pipeline" on the Run tab after installing: it analyses four small samples that come with
the program and whose contents are known, and tells you whether the right
answer came back. It takes about fifteen seconds and needs no internet
connection.

You will need a library before analysing your own data, or you can point
TaxaTag at NCBI over the internet instead, which needs no setup but is slower.
